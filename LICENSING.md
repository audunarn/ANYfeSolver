source licences, and third-party material retain their respective licences and

Packaging tools install ANYfeSolver's dependencies separately. The version
requirements and corresponding licences are listed in `dependency-licenses.json`
and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Earlier distributions of ecosystem packages
retain the licences under which their owners published them.

The 0.4.0 transition is based on the project owner's confirmation and a Git
author audit showing only owner-controlled identities plus local release and
qualification automation. No claim is made here about third-party works beyond
the notices recorded in the repository.

Documentation and datasets may move to separately scoped licences only after a
